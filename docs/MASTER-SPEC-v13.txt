===============================================================
HOTDOG DERBY
REMAINING DEVELOPMENT SPECIFICATION
===============================================================

Purpose
-------

This replaces the previous full master specification with the work that is
still required. Completed work is intentionally omitted. For implementation
history and automated checks, use FEATURE-CHECKLIST.md.

This document remains authoritative for unfinished design. Do not re-add
removed mechanics or superseded arena features.


Current design decisions that must remain
----------------------------------------

- Hotdog Derby is an eight-human-player online horse-racing party roguelike.
  Single Player is a configurable solo sandbox, not a fake AI multiplayer game.
- Players never attack, damage, stun, or knock back other players. Throwables
  affect horses, track objects, obstacles, and race conditions.
- The active inventory is exactly ten hotbar slots: Hotdog, Golden Hotdog,
  Carrot, Golden Carrot, Soda, Water Bottle, Hurdle, Beach Ball, Foam
  Horseshoe, and Chair. Do not restore the removed inventory wheel.
- The non-phone UI uses the clubhouse/lobby visual language. The phone keeps
  its own dark Hotdog OS design.
- Fixer services are always enabled. The commentator booth, horse tunnel,
  former parking lots, exterior road ring, and exterior gatehouses are removed.
- The current arena is the simplified single-bowl version. Do not restore the
  experimental stacked green/blue floors or old stair towers.
- The current supplied player model and supplied item GLBs are the source
  geometry. Preserve their triangles and proportions while improving materials,
  animation and layered cosmetics.


1. Release-critical multiplayer and hosting
-------------------------------------------

- Deploy and verify the new WebSocket transport on the selected Cloudflare
  Durable Objects free tier. Local Node transport, browser adapter, Cloudflare
  target, atomic seats, permissions, privacy and reconnect tests are complete;
  the hosted endpoint and two-device cloud playtest remain open.
- Make the host authoritative for lobby membership, rules, race state, money,
  items, bets, trades, sabotage, results, replay events and game settings.
- Synchronize host-owned lobby rules and display them live for every client.
- Add reconnect handling, host permission checks, membership checks, graceful
  network failure states, anti-duplication / anti-double-spend safeguards, and
  server-authoritative auction/trade transactions.
- Verify all of the above with two real clients, including reconnect during a
  victory/day transition, player equipment/throw replay, DerbyPay requests and
  payouts.


2. Core roguelike run
---------------------

- Complete the configurable multi-day run: preparation, buying, betting,
  racing, payouts, day results, reward choice, negative effect choice, shopping,
  auctions, legendary opportunities, trading, then next race/day.
- At each eligible day change, show standings, money, race results and major
  statistics. It must be a polished event screen, not a fabricated solo
  leaderboard.
- Offer one positive choice from each category: Money, Item and Upgrade. The
  player picks one. Upgrades are only earned from that choice; never add an
  Upgrade shop, inventory tab, equipment slots or drops.
- Offer two distinct Negative Effects and require one choice. Effects should
  create tradeoffs without making a run unplayable.
- Keep reward choices randomized, fair and meaningfully different between runs.
- Build a real economy: expensive high-power opportunities, useful cheap options
  and underdog recovery options. Add progressive upgrades unlocked by money
  progression with clear presentation and balance.
- End runs with a Championship race made from unique qualifying normal/personal
  horse winners; keep at most eight, using winning time to break over-cap ties.


3. Game settings and Single Player
----------------------------------

- Add host-synchronized multiplayer settings and connect each setting only when
  its underlying system exists. Settings include days, races, horse count, laps,
  money, crowd chaos/frequency, events/modifiers, player horses/racing, personal
  horse limits, item/legendary availability and upgrades.
- Enforce setting dependencies across gate slots, lanes, odds, payouts, camera
  framing, race logic and UI; reject invalid combinations.
- Preserve the existing Single Player setup bounds while adding usable sandbox
  support for the configured systems above. Solo has no player leaderboard or
  simulated player opponents.
- Recheck phone/setup bounds at 150% UI scale on a narrow display.


4. Items, concessions, auctions and inventory
----------------------------------------------

- Integrate the ten active item assets with their final effects, physics,
  quantities, shops and network state. Review first-person grips for every item.
- Golden Hotdog and Golden Carrot belong to the legendary economy, not normal
  concessions. Design the legendary shop and its availability/economy.
- Implement the Concessions app Auction tab: three listings, image/name/category
  /description, starting/current bid, bidder, 15-second timer and atomic winner
  settlement.
- Finish collision, bounce and placement tuning for the standardized item
  effects, and balance each effect's independent strength under multiplayer
  latency. The six effect types and three-effect maximum are implemented.
- Keep the ten-slot hotbar and automatic same-category replacement. Do not bring
  back a hold-E wheel or an inventory phone app.
- Model any remaining normal/legendary assets only when explicitly added back to
  the active catalog.


5. Phone apps and social systems
--------------------------------

- Finish a consistent, polished phone home screen, icons and unique app layouts.
- Concessions must resemble a real food-ordering app and retain 12-second phone
  delivery. Physical stands remain instant pickup with valid shared pricing.
- Finish Betting with a distinct racing identity and WIN / PLACE / SHOW / EXACTA
  / TRIFECTA flows, ticket previews, locked payouts and live status.
- Finish Horse Stats: current field, all discovered horses, and Personal Horse
  tabs with correct unavailable states.
- Complete DerbyPay send/request money, send/request item and atomic trades with
  explicit confirmation by both parties. Verify this with real clients.
- Complete dynamic DerbyNews: leaders, odds, hits, stuns, overtakes, payouts,
  sabotage, rare events, auctions and player-race news.
- Complete the Sabotage app with costs, targets, risk, detection, active history
  and result feedback. Supported options must remain legible and balanced.
- Keep Messages input safe: typing must never trigger gameplay shortcuts.


6. Player character, cosmetics and personal horses
---------------------------------------------------

- Finish review of the supplied-base player under real game lighting. Correct
  elbow/knee deformation, hands against held props, walking, phone and throwing
  animation without decimating or replacing the original separate pieces.
- Confirm material matching with the user on their display. Review newly reported
  item-model mismatches as they arise.
- Expand cosmetics: hats, shirts, pants, shoes, accessories, body colors, emotes
  and phone themes. Keep cosmetics separate from gameplay upgrades.
- Implement host-authoritative personal horse ownership, archetype choice,
  naming validation/filtering, cosmetics, entries, histories and race limits.
- Build ten balanced archetypes: Sprinter, Tank, Endurance, Rocket, Smart,
  Agile, Power, Comeback, Balanced and Wildcard.
- Give eligible player horses a small configurable max-speed advantage, make them
  non-bettable by opponents, show clear PLAYER HORSE identity, and implement
  entry/odds/payout updates.
- Implement player-controlled racing: accelerate, brake, steer and jump, plus
  entry cost/payout. Non-racing players may still bet, throw, sabotage and emote;
  players are never attack targets.


7. Horses, odds and race simulation
-----------------------------------

- Finish horse/jockey geometry, final rig and speed-scaled animation. Continue
  to preserve the improved articulated gait rather than changing the supplied
  horse shape arbitrarily.
- Calibrate quoted opening chances against repeated simulated races; locked
  ticket odds and payout must match actual settlement. Verify with two clients.
- Preserve active-field-only odds, random field selection and separated strength
  groups; never allow a six-horse all-5:1 book again.
- Complete AI for momentum, lane changes, overtaking, avoidance of stopped horses,
  effects, obstacles, stamina, cornering, intelligence and personality.
- Keep 4–8 lane/gate layouts correct in every horse count and ensure camera
  framing, numbers, odds and payouts update with the field.


8. Arena, locations, sound and performance
-------------------------------------------

- Perform remaining arena reference polish: manually traverse every seat row,
  stair approach, glass panel, booth approach and vendor interaction in-game.
  Seats must only be removed for true stair footprints, and no booth may clip
  exterior glass.
- Continue stadium-life details that do not harm frame time: crowd, workers,
  vendors, posters, flags, plants, signs, cameras and reserved seating.
- Complete three location themes while retaining recognizable stadium structure:
  Classic, Mountain and Desert. Use clean, layered horizons—not placeholder
  mountains—and give gameplay music a matching identity.
- Profile the game on integrated graphics and on the powerful PC. Investigate the
  intermittent performanceOats fingerprint mismatch and full asset rebuild
  fingerprint failure before release.
- Benchmark sustained 60-fps trailer capture on the powerful PC.


9. Replay, highlights and presentation
--------------------------------------

- Profile DerbyNews GPU readback on integrated graphics and lower its preview
  rate/size only if needed.
- Synchronize replay cuts, captions and event selection from the host so all
  clients see the same highlight sequence.
- Tune obstruction-aware camera selection and highlight selection during long,
  busy races. Add a compact post-race highlight index once synchronization exists.
- Keep live TV/news following the leader unless a nearby impactful event earns a
  replay. Preserve the projectile-follow camera for strong hits.
- Finish remaining lobby flow, accessibility, control-remapping and controller
  focus/glyph validation. Verify platform-specific Quit behavior for Steam.


10. Release gates
-----------------

Before calling the game release-ready, complete and verify:

- Two-client networking, reconnects, transactions, bets, payouts, player replay
  and host authority.
- Full run loop, rewards/effects, progression, economy, auction and Championship.
- Personal horses and player-controlled races.
- Calibrated odds and finished horse/jockey assets/animation.
- Final asset, phone, UI-scale, controller and accessibility reviews.
- Arena traversal/polish, all three locations, audio pass and performance targets.
- Extended replay/highlight test sessions and clean trailer capture.


Implementation rules
--------------------

- Keep tests updated when a system changes. Run the relevant focused tests and
  the full suite before marking a release gate complete.
- Treat real two-client/manual visual testing as required where automated tests
  cannot prove behavior.
- Update FEATURE-CHECKLIST.md as tasks become complete or need further checks.
